Motion Design Services

Promo videos, logo animation and scroll-stopping ad creatives — motion that makes brands impossible to ignore.

What's included

  • Promo Videos

    Launch films, product teasers and brand videos built around a clear message — from script and storyboard to final sound-ready cut.

  • Social Ad Creatives

    Thumb-stopping vertical and square creatives for Meta, TikTok and YouTube — hooks in the first second, offer on screen, CTA at the end.

  • Logo Animation & Stings

    Kinetic logos, intros and outros that give your brand a recognisable move across videos, streams and presentations.

  • Explainer Videos

    Complex products explained simply — kinetic typography, infographics in motion and clean visual storytelling.

  • UI & Product Motion

    Interface animations, product walkthroughs and feature highlights that make apps and websites feel premium.

  • Video Editing & Post

    Editing, pacing, captions, sound sync and colour polish for raw footage — ready to publish on any platform.

Motion design questions

How much does motion design cost?
Pricing depends on length, style, and complexity. A short logo animation costs less than a full promo video or campaign kit. Contact us with your idea and we will prepare a custom quote.
How long does a motion design project take?
Simple animations take days, while promo videos and campaign systems usually take 2–6 weeks including concept, production, and 1–2 revision rounds.
What do you need from me to start?
Just a goal — a detailed brief, a rough idea, or references are all enough. We help shape the concept, style, and structure before production starts.
What formats will I receive?
Final videos in MP4/WebM plus platform-ready exports: vertical for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, square for feeds, and horizontal for YouTube and websites.
