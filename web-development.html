Web development questions

How much does a landing page cost?
The cost of a landing page depends on its design complexity, number of sections, animations, integrations and content requirements. A simple landing page takes significantly less time to develop than a highly interactive, conversion-focused website with custom animations and integrations. Contact us with your requirements for an accurate estimate.
React or Next.js — which should I choose?
React is a strong choice for interactive web applications and custom frontend experiences. Next.js extends React with server-side rendering, static generation and application-level optimizations. For SEO-focused websites, business websites and performance-sensitive projects, Next.js is often the better choice.
How long does website development take?
Development time depends on the scope and complexity of the project. A focused landing page takes significantly less time than a multi-page business website, e-commerce platform or custom web application. The timeline also depends on design readiness, content, integrations and revisions.
Do you redesign existing websites?
Yes. We redesign and modernize existing websites, improve their responsive behavior, rebuild interfaces from existing designs or Figma files, and optimize performance and usability without necessarily rebuilding every part of the project from scratch.
Can you convert a Figma design into a website?
Yes. We convert Figma designs into responsive, pixel-perfect web interfaces using modern frontend technologies such as React, Next.js, TypeScript, HTML and CSS.
Can you integrate APIs into a website?
Yes. We develop and integrate REST APIs for websites and web applications, including communication between frontend interfaces, backend services, databases and third-party systems.
Do you build responsive websites?
Yes. Responsive development is part of our standard workflow. Websites are designed and developed to work across desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes while maintaining usability, visual consistency and performance.
